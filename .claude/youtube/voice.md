# voice.md — SPACE SECRETS

Written from the channel's own scripts (Black Hole EP01, Earth Stopped Spinning EP02, Glass Rain EP03,
Sun Warm-up, Enceladus EP04, and the YouTube Moon documentary). Canonical copy: `.claude/youtube/voice.md`
in the repo; copy it to `~/.claude/youtube/voice.md` at session start.

## Who I am talking to
One person: a curious 16–35-year-old in the US, scrolling TikTok or YouTube Shorts at night on their phone,
usually with the sound off at first. They like space, have no physics background, and will swipe in under
2 seconds unless something is happening on screen. They're not a scientist, but they can smell clickbait
and they hate being lied to.

## How I actually talk
Short spoken sentences. Second person ("you'd be stretched", "imagine looking up tonight"). A dramatic claim,
then the honest qualifier straight after. One-word beats for rhythm ("Water. Salts. Organic compounds.").
A turn signposted with "But here's the twist" / "But here's the most important part" / "And then it gets stranger".
Ends on an open question ("Could something be alive down there?", "Would we survive?").

Excerpts (real lines from the channel):
- "Fall into a black hole... and to everyone watching, you'd never quite make it in."
- "For about eight minutes, Earth wouldn't even know. Then suddenly... darkness."
- "On this planet, the rain could tear you apart. Because it might be made of glass."
- "This moon is shooting its ocean into space! And NASA actually flew a spacecraft through the spray."
- "Scientists have NOT discovered aliens there."
- "If the Moon disappeared tonight, humanity might survive. But Earth would never be quite the same again."

## Words I never use
"Unlock", "game-changer", "dive in", "let's get into it", "mind-blowing", "insane", "you won't believe",
"guys", "welcome back", "smash that like", "secret they don't want you to know", "100% proof", "aliens found",
"NASA confirms" (unless NASA actually confirmed it).

## Words I do use
"Imagine...", "Here's the twist", "And then it gets stranger", "actually", "scientists believe",
"a scientific possibility", "NOT", "frozen", "hidden", "nobody knows", "Could ... ?"

## What I will not claim
- Life on other worlds (no "aliens found"). Only "ingredients", "conditions", "places to search".
- Hypotheticals as fact. A "what if" is always labelled HYPOTHETICAL on screen.
- CGI as real footage. Every animation carries SIMULATION / VISUALIZATION / CGI RECONSTRUCTION.
- Numbers without a source (NASA, ESA, NOAA, peer-reviewed papers). If a figure isn't sourced, the line goes without it.
- Instant-doom outcomes the science doesn't support (instant tsunamis, the Earth "freezing instantly").

## My format
- TikTok/Shorts: 15–90 s, 1080×1920, no intro, no logo up front, the visual event on frame 0, the narrator speaking by 0.1 s,
  kinetic captions (Inter Black, white plus yellow #FFD400, black stroke), AI narrator (Kokoro am_puck, American male),
  original CGI, mixed to -14 LUFS. Follows SPACE_SECRETS_RETENTION_SYSTEM.md.
- YouTube long-form: 11–13 min, 1920×1080, chapters, small documentary subtitles, chapter cards, end screen
  "SPACE SECRETS — EXPLORE THE UNKNOWN."
- No face on camera. No swearing. A calm, suspenseful tone, never shouting.
