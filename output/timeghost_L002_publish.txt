DESCRIPTION
-----------
Most people think slaves built the pyramids. Or aliens. Fardin went back 4,500 years to see for himself.

Fardin's time-travel watch drops him on the Giza plateau as a ghost, in the reign of the pharaoh Khufu. Nobody can see him, hear him or touch him. He watches the Great Pyramid rise from the quarry floor to the gleaming white tip:
- copper chisels cutting limestone at Tura
- boats carrying stone down the Nile, recorded in the oldest written papyri ever found
- the water trick that halves the force needed to drag a two-ton block
- the workers' town where they baked bread, brewed beer and ate a lot of meat
- the clue that settled the slavery question for most archaeologists

Every fact in this episode is based on published research. Where scholars still argue (how the blocks were raised, for example), we tell you.
