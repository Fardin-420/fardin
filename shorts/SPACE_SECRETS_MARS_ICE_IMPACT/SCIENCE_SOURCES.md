## Required framing
- **Frozen water, not liquid:** the video says "It's frozen, not liquid".
- **Not filmed:** the impact was never filmed or observed live. Every impact shot is labelled "RECONSTRUCTION · not real footage", and the narration says "no one saw the impact happen".
- **No invented observations:**
  - Every real image carries a "REAL NASA IMAGE · PIA#####" tag and its credit line.
  - CGI and schematic shots are labelled as such.
  - No NASA observation was invented or altered. The real images were only cropped, scaled down, and given overlay circles and labels.
- **Extent unknown:** how far the ice extends is not known. The video says "how far it spreads is still unknown".

## Schematic and rounded elements
- **Latitude map:** a schematic (procedural surface, correct 35.1°N latitude of the crater from HiRISE metadata), labelled "schematic map, not to scale".
- **Cross-sections:** the crater cross-section is to scale (150 × 21 m). The ice-layer cross-section is illustrative, labelled "layer thickness illustrative".
