All were downloaded from the NASA Image and Video Library (images-assets.nasa.gov). ## Original material
- **Impact reconstruction and terrain:** procedural CGI (generated terrain, meteoroid streak, flash, ejecta), labelled "RECONSTRUCTION · not real footage". The terrain is not a real Mars location.
- **Diagrams and maps:** the surface-wave diagram, crater and ice cross-sections, latitude map and resource icons are original schematics and illustrations.
- **Voice:** Kokoro-82M (Apache-2.0), voice am_puck (American-English male), run locally.
- **Music and sound effects:** the music, rumble and impacts were synthesized in code. They are artistic; no real Mars audio was used, and InSight's sonified data was not used.
- **Fonts:** Inter and JetBrains Mono (SIL OFL 1.1).

## Not used
- **Higher-resolution HiRISE frames:** the PDS products of the ice image were not used. Photojournal and science.nasa.gov stay blocked on this machine, and the HiRISE follow-up images are from 2023–2024, after the ice had changed.
