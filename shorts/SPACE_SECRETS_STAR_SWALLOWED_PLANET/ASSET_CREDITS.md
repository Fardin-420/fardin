# Asset credits
- **Imagery:** all imagery is original procedural CGI rendered for this video at native 1080×1920. No NASA, ESA or STScI images were used or imitated. No real Webb images of this event exist; Webb's results are spectra.
- **Star:** procedural photosphere with a 6144×3072 granulation map, plus a corona model.
- **Gas giant:** procedural 4096×2048 banded texture. Not a real image of the planet.
- **Star fields and Milky Way:** procedural plates and a 3D particle galaxy model.
- **JWST:** a simplified geometric reconstruction, labelled "CGI reconstruction · not NASA imagery".
- **Voice:** Kokoro-82M (Apache-2.0), voice am_puck.
- **Music and sound effects:** synthesized in code for this video.
