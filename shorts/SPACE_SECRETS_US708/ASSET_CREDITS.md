# Asset credits
- **Imagery:** all original procedural CGI, diagrams and schematics, rendered for this video at native 1080×1920. No NASA, ESA or Keck images were used or imitated. US 708 has never been resolved into a disk, and the supernova was never observed.
- **Milky Way:** a 3D particle model (~420,000 particles, two log-spiral arms, a bulge and dust lanes). It is a model, not a photograph.
- **US 708:** a procedural photosphere (from the 6144×3072 granulation map), turned blue-white. The size is artistic (labelled "star not to scale").
- **White dwarf, helium stream and supernova ejecta:** procedural (multi-scale ridged-noise filaments), labelled as scientific reconstruction.
- **Black-hole view:** simulated point-mass lensing of a procedural star plate, labelled "simulated lensing view · not an observation".
- **Voice:** Kokoro-82M (Apache-2.0), voice am_puck (American-English male), run locally.
- **Music, ambience and sound effects:** synthesized in code. The explosion sounds are artistic; space is silent.
- **Fonts:** Inter (SIL OFL 1.1), JetBrains Mono (SIL OFL 1.1).
