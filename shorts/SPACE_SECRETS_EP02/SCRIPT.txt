ASSUMPTIONS OF THE THOUGHT EXPERIMENT (shown on screen and in the description)
- Hypothetical: Earth cannot actually stop like this. Only the solid surface halts; the atmosphere and oceans keep
  their rotational motion at first.
- Physics (established): rotation speed, inertia, and the dependence on latitude.
- Consequences (speculative, labelled as such): wind and ocean effects are illustrated, not predicted. No casualty
  figures, no tsunami heights, and no claim that effects are the same everywhere.
