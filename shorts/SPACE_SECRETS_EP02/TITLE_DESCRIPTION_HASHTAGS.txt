What if Earth's surface suddenly stopped spinning for just five seconds, while the air and oceans kept moving? At the
equator, the ground carries you east at about 1,670 km/h. Stop the ground, and that motion doesn't simply vanish.
Inertia decides what happens next, and it depends on where you are and how suddenly the stop happens.
