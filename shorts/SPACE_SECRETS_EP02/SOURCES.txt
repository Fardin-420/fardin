3. SPECULATIVE CONSEQUENCES (illustrated, not predicted)
- Extreme eastward winds relative to the ground: follows from inertia under the stated assumption. Real-world
  severity would depend on friction, terrain, altitude and the (impossible) stopping mechanism, so it is not quantified
  beyond the equatorial upper bound.
- Ocean water piling against west-facing coasts and withdrawing from east-facing ones: the direction follows from
  inertia (water keeps moving east). Heights and inland reach are explicitly NOT given ("HOW HIGH? UNCERTAIN").
- Restart "jolt": an abrupt return to 464 m/s is the same magnitude of velocity change. Effects are not quantified.
No casualty estimates are made, and effects are never said to be uniform; latitude dependence is shown explicitly.
