SCIENCE / HONESTY CHECK
- The hypothetical is framed on screen ("HYPOTHETICAL SIMULATION · not real footage", story clock "t = x.x s / 5 s")
  and in the description. The surface stops; the atmosphere and oceans retain their motion, exactly as specified.
- Physical principles (rotation speed, cos-latitude dependence, inertia, acceleration figures) are separated from
  speculative consequences (wind and ocean effects, shown as illustrative). No casualty estimates, no tsunami
  heights, no claim of uniform global effects.
- Numbers: 1,670 km/h (NASA Earth Fact Sheet, derived); 408 km/h record gust (WMO archive); 2.3 km, 47 g and 0.013 g
  derived (see SOURCES.txt).
- No NASA footage is claimed. The Earth imagery is Solar System Scope (CC BY 4.0, NASA-based), credited on screen.

DEFECTS FOUND AND FIXED DURING QC
1. Coastal surge masks: 450 km flat slabs and island streaks -> island removal (morphological opening), 1.8° fading
   band, Gaussian soft edge.
2. Top tag collided with the channel brand -> brand moved to the second row.
3. Opening title overflowed the frame width -> resized.
4. Atlantic close-up cut Brazil off -> reframed (centre 22°W, R 1250) so both coasts are in frame.
5. Wind and ocean streaks too faint for phones -> thicker and brighter.
6. Opening read as empty black space (and tripped cropdetect) -> denser starfield + faint galactic haze.
7. Thin dark seam along the planet's limb in every scene -> halo blended on both sides of the limb; full re-render.

KNOWN LIMITATIONS AND RISKS (honest)
- NASA/NOAA hosts were blocked by the environment's network policy, so sources couldn't be re-opened in-session and
  no NASA imagery or footage could be used. The Earth is rendered from CC BY textures instead.
- No licensed music library was reachable; the score is original and synthesized (same engine as EP01).
- Rotation is sped up for visibility (labelled). Wind and ocean visuals are illustrative, not fluid simulations.
- Caption word timing is estimated from audio energy (no ASR model available): phrase-accurate; single words may be
  off by ~0.1–0.2 s.
- I can't listen to audio here; mix balance and voice naturalness were verified by measurement only. A human listen
  is recommended before publishing.
- No guarantee against platform claims; disclose the AI voice where asked.
