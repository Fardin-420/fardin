## Notes on the JIRAM imagery
- **Native resolution is low.** NASA's PIA26527 is a large annotated product, so it looks soft at any size. That softness is a property of the data, not of the edit.

## Original material
- **Juno spacecraft:** CGI reconstruction, labelled.
- **Diagrams and simulations:** area squares, power bars, saturation meter, eruption cluster, magma-chamber cross-section, resonance, eccentric orbit and tidal profile.
- **Voice:** Kokoro-82M (Apache-2.0), voice am_puck.
- **Music and sound effects:** original, synthesized in code (impact, thermal-scan sweep, ticks). No recorded or "fake Io" sounds.
- **Fonts:** Inter and JetBrains Mono (SIL OFL 1.1).

## Cover
- **Source:** PIA26527, scaled down.
- **Bloom:** a soft glow derived from its own bright pixels was added for legibility on the feed.
- **Text:** the headline only.
