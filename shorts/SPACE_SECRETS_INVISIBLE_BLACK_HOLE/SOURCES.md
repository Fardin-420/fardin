## Visualization honesty
- **Lensing:** shown hugely exaggerated. The real Einstein radius is ~5 milliarcseconds (Mróz et al. quote 5.18 ± 0.51 mas), unresolvable in any image. Labelled on screen.
- **No accretion disk** is drawn around OGLE-2011-BLG-0462: none is observed. The only disk shown is a generic black-hole binary, labelled "not OGLE-2011-BLG-0462".
- **Light curve and centroid path:** a textbook point-lens model, shape only, labelled "simplified". Hubble cutouts are simulated, with the shift exaggerated ~10,000×.
- **Imagery:** no NASA images were used, and none are imitated. Hubble and the observatory are labelled CGI reconstructions.
- **Close-pass simulation:** velocity-Verlet N-body (Sun + Earth, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune + a 7 M☉ black hole at 30 km/s, closest ~35 AU). Illustrative; not a prediction.
