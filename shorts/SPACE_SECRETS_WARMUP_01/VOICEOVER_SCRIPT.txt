- Hypothetical: the Sun can't vanish (mass-energy is conserved). This is labelled on screen the whole time.
- Light takes ~8 min 20 s (499 s) to cross 1 AU. Changes in gravity also travel at light speed (confirmed by
  GW170817), so Earth keeps its orbit AND its daylight for the same ~8 min 20 s. The countdown runs to 8:20.
- After that, Earth would move off along a straight line at its orbital speed (~29.8 km/s).
- The video does NOT say Earth freezes instantly or that humanity dies right away: it ends on an open question.
