FACT SOURCES (research only — no media taken)
- NASA/JPL, "Pale Blue Dot" image release (PIA00452) and the 30th-anniversary "Pale Blue Dot Revisited" (2020):
  taken Feb 14, 1990 by Voyager 1 from about 6 billion km (3.7 billion miles); Earth is a point of light about
  0.12 pixel in size; the light bands are sunlight scattered in the camera optics because Earth was close to the Sun
  from Voyager's viewpoint; part of the "Family Portrait" mosaic, the last images Voyager 1 took before its cameras
  were powered off to conserve power and memory for the rest of the mission.
  https://photojournal.jpl.nasa.gov/catalog/PIA00452
  https://www.jpl.nasa.gov/images/pia23645-pale-blue-dot-revisited/
- The idea of turning the cameras back toward Earth is credited to Carl Sagan (paraphrased in the closing line,
  not quoted).
