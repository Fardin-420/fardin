ORIGINAL COMMENTARY VALUE
Explains when and where the photo was taken, how small Earth really was in it (a fraction of one pixel), why it sits
in a beam of light (sunlight scattered inside the camera, because Earth was close to the Sun from Voyager's view),
and why it was a final act (the cameras were powered off to save power). It closes on the perspective the image is
famous for.
