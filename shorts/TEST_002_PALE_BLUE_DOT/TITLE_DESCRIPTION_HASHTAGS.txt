This Tiny Dot Is Every Human Who Ever Lived

TITLE (A/B alternates)
Voyager 1's Last Photo Was Less Than One Pixel
The Most Humbling Photo Ever Taken

DESCRIPTION
On February 14, 1990, Voyager 1 turned its camera back toward home from about 3.7 billion miles away. Earth showed up
as a point of light just 0.12 of a pixel across, sitting in a band of sunlight scattered inside the camera. Soon
after, Voyager's cameras were switched off for good; these were the last images it ever took. This is the "Pale Blue
Dot".

Visuals in this Short are original illustrations, not the NASA image. Facts: NASA/JPL (PIA00452; "Pale Blue Dot
Revisited", 2020). Narration: AI-generated voice (Kokoro TTS).
