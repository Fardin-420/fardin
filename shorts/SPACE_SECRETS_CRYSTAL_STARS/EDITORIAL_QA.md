- **Gaia:** the diagram is labelled "SCHEMATIC after Tremblay et al. 2019 · not the actual Gaia data". The spacecraft is labelled "CGI RECONSTRUCTION · not ESA imagery".
- **Not diamonds:** "Not a gemstone you could hold" (narration and on screen). No diamond or jewellery imagery appears.
- **Dates:** 2019 (Gaia, Nature) and 2024 (Nature). No 2026 discovery is claimed.
- **Scope:** "in some white dwarfs" for distillation. The ending card says "not every white dwarf crystallizes the same way".
