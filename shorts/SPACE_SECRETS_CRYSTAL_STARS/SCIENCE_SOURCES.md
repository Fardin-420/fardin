# Science sources — "Some Dead Stars Turn Into Crystals"
The primary sites (ESA, Nature, arXiv) are blocked from this build machine's network, so each claim was checked against search-indexed copies of the sources below.

## 1. Gaia evidence for core crystallization (Jan 9, 2019)
- **Paper:** Tremblay, P.-E. et al. 2019, "Core crystallization and pile-up in the cooling sequence of evolving white dwarfs", *Nature* 565, 202–205, published 9 January 2019.
- **Releases:** ESA, "Gaia reveals how Sun-like stars turn solid after their demise"; University of Warwick news release.
- **Supports these lines:**
  - Gaia DR2 photometry and parallaxes of about 15,000 white dwarfs within ~100 pc of the Sun.
  - A pile-up in the cooling sequence, explained by latent heat released when the carbon–oxygen core crystallizes. That heat slows the cooling.
  - Some white dwarfs can appear up to ~2 billion years younger than they are.
  - Reported as the first direct observational evidence of white-dwarf crystallization.
- **On screen:** the evidence is statistical, from colours and brightnesses of many stars. No one has imaged a white dwarf's interior. The pile-up diagram is a schematic labelled "after Tremblay et al. 2019 · not the actual Gaia data".

## 2. Buoyant crystals delay cooling (2024)
- **Paper:** Bédard, A., Blouin, S. & Cheng, S. 2024, "Buoyant crystals halt the cooling of white dwarf stars", *Nature* 627, 286–288 (March 2024).
- **Supports these lines:**
  - In some white dwarfs, the solid crystals that form are lighter than the surrounding liquid and float upward.
  - Heavier material (neon-22-rich) sinks. This is "distillation".
  - The separation releases gravitational energy and can stall cooling for billions of years.
  - It may explain a population of anomalously "delayed" white dwarfs.
- **Framing:** "in some white dwarfs", not all.

## 3. Background physics (textbook)
- **Origin:** a Sun-like star ends as a red giant, sheds its envelope (planetary nebula) and leaves a white dwarf.
- **Mass and size:** a typical white dwarf has ~0.6 solar masses ("about half the Sun's mass") and a radius comparable to Earth's.
- **Density:** mean density is ~10⁶ g/cm³, about a million times that of water, so a teaspoon (~5 cm³) weighs several tonnes. The script says "several tons".
- **Structure:** the core is mostly carbon and oxygen. At these densities the ions (nuclei) form a Coulomb lattice (a body-centred cubic crystal), while degenerate electrons move freely.

## Claims deliberately avoided
- "Diamond star" or gemstone language. The script and graphics say "not a gemstone"; the crystal is ionised C/O matter, not transparent diamond.
- Discovery dated 2026. Crystallization was predicted in the 1960s; the Gaia evidence is from 2019 and the distillation result from 2024.
- "Every white dwarf crystallizes the same way". The ending card says this is not the case.
- Any fake Gaia image or data plot.
