## Corrections to the brief
1. **"First color photos from another planet's surface" is false.** NASA's Viking 1 lander sent colour images from Mars in July 1976. The script says "the first color pictures from the surface **of Venus**".
2. **"Measured at the site":** the commonly cited Venera 13 readings are 457 °C and 89 atm. The narration rounds these to "about 460 °C" and "about 90 times".

## Optional detail not used
- **Venera 14 lens-cap story** (the soil-compressibility probe landed on an ejected lens cap): only secondary sources support it (Wikipedia, popular articles). No primary source was found, so it was left out, as the brief asked.

## Microphone caveat
- Some sources say it's unclear how much of the acoustic data reached Earth intact. The narration only says the microphone "listened to the wind", which the 1982 paper supports. It doesn't claim we can play back "the sound of Venus".
