SPACE SECRETS — VENUS KILLED EVERY LANDER — THREE HOOK OPTIONS (Rule 2)
Scores from hookscore.py (/yt-script). Every option was fact-checked.

HOOK A — shocking fact (MANDATED, CHOSEN)
  Narration: "This planet killed every machine we ever landed on it. The toughest one lasted 127 minutes.
              But before it died, it sent you a picture of this."
  Score: 56 WORKABLE. The mandated wording "...it sent you this." scores 58. The words "a picture of" were added because
         the shot it points to is a labelled reconstruction, not the real photo.
  First frame: a glowing Venera-type lander already plunging through thick yellow clouds; hook caption
               "VENUS KILLED EVERY LANDER".
  Camera: descending with the lander, cloud layers streaming up past it in parallax; slight shake.
  Sound: bass impact plus whoosh at 0.00 s; voice at 0.00 s.
  On-screen text: VENUS KILLED EVERY LANDER (one text block, 4 words)
  Why viewers stay: a death sentence for machines, a number (127 min), and a promised image ("a picture of this"),
                    paid off at 6.3-7.5 s.
  Fact check: PASS. Every Venus lander (Venera 7-14, Vega 1-2, Pioneer Venus probes) failed within about 2 hours.
              Venera 13 lasted 127 min, the record.

  Score: 34 WEAK.

  Fact check: FAILS for this production. The real photo could not be used (licence unclear, host unreachable), so the

  Verdict: rejected. Its idea is reused as the 6.3 s payoff (line-scan reveal of the reconstructed view).

DECISION: A (mandated). Neither B nor C scored higher, so no check-in was needed before rendering.
