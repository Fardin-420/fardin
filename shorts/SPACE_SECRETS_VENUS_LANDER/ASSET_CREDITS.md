## Real Venera photos: **not used**
- **Licence unclear:** sources conflict. Wikimedia Commons calls them public domain under a NASA rationale that doesn't fit a Soviet-made image. NSSDCA mirrors say "public domain", while the Smithsonian Air & Space entry lists NASA JPL as rights holder.
- **Host blocked:** the NSSDCA host isn't on this environment's network allowlist.
- **What was used instead:** the surface view is an **original reconstruction** modelled on the published Venera 13 panorama: curved horizon, flat rock slabs, the lander's toothed ring and colour chart. Every frame showing it is labelled "RECONSTRUCTION of Venera 13's 1982 view · not the original photo".

## Original material
- **CGI and diagrams:** lander, cloud descent, surface reconstruction, cutaway, thermometer and lead block, ocean-depth scale, timeline and clock graphics.
- **Earth:** a procedural globe built from the 4096×2048 texture set used in earlier Space Secrets videos.
- **Ocean-Venus:** an artist's impression made by tinting the low plains of the Magellan map. Labelled HYPOTHESIS and artist's impression.
- **Voice:** Kokoro-82M (Apache-2.0), voice am_puck, run locally.
- **Music and sound effects:** synthesized in code. The impact, rumble, ticks and static are artistic; no real Venus audio was used.
- **Fonts:** Inter and JetBrains Mono (SIL OFL 1.1).
