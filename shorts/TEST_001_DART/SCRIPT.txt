ORIGINAL COMMENTARY VALUE
The narration explains what the mission was, why the camera feed ends abruptly (the spacecraft was destroyed on
impact), how success was measured (telescopes timing the moonlet's orbit), and why it matters (first deliberate change
to a celestial body's motion — the first planetary-defense test). None of that is visible in the images alone.

SCRIPT REVISION LOG
v1 closing line said "the first time humans EVER changed how a celestial body moves". Corrected to "deliberately":
NASA's claim is the first time humanity *purposely* changed a celestial object's motion (Deep Impact struck comet
Tempel 1 in 2005 with an immeasurably small effect). Line re-voiced and re-rendered.
