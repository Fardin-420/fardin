TITLE (primary)
NASA Crashed a Spacecraft Into an Asteroid… On Purpose

TITLE (A/B alternates)
The Asteroid's Orbit Changed by 32 Minutes
Why NASA Smashed DART Into an Asteroid

DESCRIPTION
In 2022, NASA's DART spacecraft flew straight into Dimorphos, a 160-meter moonlet orbiting the asteroid Didymos, at
about 14,000 mph. Its camera sent back one image per second until the moment of impact. Weeks later, telescopes
confirmed the result: Dimorphos's orbit got 32 minutes shorter. It was the first time humanity deliberately changed
how a celestial body moves, and the world's first planetary-defense test. (Neither asteroid was ever a threat to
Earth.)

Visuals in this Short are original illustrations, not mission footage. Facts: NASA (Oct 11, 2022 release; DART
mission pages). Narration: AI-generated voice (Kokoro TTS).
