## On-screen numbers
- **"~700–1,000 °C, momentarily":** the formation temperature range proposed by Liu et al. 2025 (source 3).
- **"Chang'e-6 · far-side samples returned June 2024":** source 4.
- **Earth's apparent size from the Moon (~1.9°):** used to size Earth correctly for each lens in the telephoto shots (standard geometry: 12,742 km at ~384,400 km).
- **Final wide shot:** Earth and Moon sizes to scale (Moon/Earth radius = 0.273); distance compressed (labelled on screen).

- The full texts of refs 1–3 (jpl.nasa.gov, PMC, PubMed, science.org and arXiv are blocked in this environment). Confirmation is from consistent search-engine records, university and press releases, and science-news coverage.
