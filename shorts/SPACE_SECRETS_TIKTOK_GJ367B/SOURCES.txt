CORE REFERENCES (pre-2026)
 1. Lam, K. W. F. et al. (2021), "GJ 367b: A dense, ultrashort-period sub-Earth planet transiting a nearby red dwarf
    star", Science 374, 1271. Discovery with TESS + HARPS: orbital period ≈ 7.7 h (0.32 d), radius ≈ 0.72 R⊕,
    dayside up to ~1,500 °C.
 2. Goffo, E. et al. (2023), ApJ Letters 955, L3. Refined mass ≈ 0.63 M⊕ and radius ≈ 0.70 R⊕; a very high
    density implying an iron core of roughly 90% of the mass ("super-Mercury"). This is the "EARLIER ESTIMATE" in the
    video, and is what popular coverage turned into "nearly pure iron".
 3. Zhang, M. et al. (2024), ApJ Letters (JWST MIRI). Dayside thermal emission is consistent with a dark, bare rock with
    no thick atmosphere, which supports the airless dark-rock reconstruction and the hot dayside.
