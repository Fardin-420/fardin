SCIENCE ACCURACY REVIEW
Gravitational lensing   Computed, not drawn: real Schwarzschild null geodesics show the disk's far side above the
                        shadow, the photon ring and the distorted starfield.
Event horizon           Presented as a boundary ("not a surface", "point of no return", escape > c), with a
                        light-cone diagram.
Tidal forces            Defined as a difference in pull. Stellar-mass case stretches outside the horizon;
                        supermassive case crosses intact.
Time dilation           Framed explicitly as the distant observer's view; the faller's clock is normal.
Speculation             The center is presented as unknown. No wormhole, white-hole or "see the future" claims.
Real vs simulated       Every frame is tagged SIMULATION / DIAGRAM. The EHT image is named as real and as "not
                        shown"; the blurred ring is labelled as a simulation.
