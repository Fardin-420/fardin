How the simulation was made (tools/raytrace.py): non-rotating (Schwarzschild) black hole; null geodesics traced
backwards per pixel by integrating d²u/dφ² = 3u² − u (u = 1/r, G = M = c = 1) with RK4; disk from the innermost stable
orbit r = 6M to 22M; Keplerian orbital speed; relativistic Doppler factor plus gravitational redshift drive brightness
and color (approximate); procedural starfield lensed by the same geodesics. Simplifications: no spin (real M87* and
Sgr A* are thought to spin), thin opaque disk, static/hovering camera (a free-falling camera would see aberration),
artistic disk turbulence and colors.
