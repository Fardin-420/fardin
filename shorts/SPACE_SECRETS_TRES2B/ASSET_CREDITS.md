# Asset credits
- **Imagery:** all original procedural CGI, illustrations and schematics, rendered for this video at native 1080×1920. No NASA, Kepler or observatory images were used or imitated. No image of TrES-2b exists; it has only been studied through starlight measurements.
- **TrES-2b:** a procedural banded gas giant (4096×2048 band texture), drawn near-black with a dull-red thermal glow and a thin backlit atmospheric rim. It is an artist's impression with sizes not to scale.
- **Host star:** a procedural photosphere (6144×3072 granulation map).
- **Kepler telescope:** a simplified geometric reconstruction, labelled "CGI reconstruction · not NASA imagery".
- **Coal, paint card and stove coil:** procedural illustrations.
- **Voice:** Kokoro-82M (Apache-2.0), voice am_puck (American-English male), run locally.
- **Music, ambience, measurement tones and impacts:** synthesized in code. They are artistic, not recorded sounds from space.
- **Fonts:** Inter (SIL OFL 1.1) and JetBrains Mono (SIL OFL 1.1).
