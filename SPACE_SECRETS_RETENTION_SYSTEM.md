# SPACE SECRETS — Viral Retention System V3 (standing rules)

Applies to **every new Space Secrets TikTok / Short**. Adopted after the 70 s black-hole video had 12 % average retention,
with the biggest drop at 0:01.

## Lessons from the black-hole video (don't repeat)
- Frame 0 was a white flash fading in, which reads as a grey, empty frame on the feed.
- The opening was a near-static black hole with a slow zoom: no visual event and no human subject.
- Two competing text blocks (a title card plus word captions).
- The first line was abstract and took 4.4 s ("Fall into a black hole... and to everyone watching, you'd never quite make it in.").
- The second line sounded like a disclaimer ("This is a simulation...").
- The emotional payoff (the viewer being stretched) didn't arrive until 0:25–0:40.

## Rule 1 — The first frame must be unmissable
Never open with: a static planet or black-hole image, a slow zoom into space, a logo or intro, a fade from black **or
white**, generic stars or an empty background, a slow narrator intro, or a text-only title card.

Open on the most visually compelling, scientifically relevant moment. Within the first 0.5 s:
- a striking, recognizable **visual event** (already in motion on frame 0)
- the narrator **already speaking** an irresistible statement (VO starts ≤ 0.1 s)
- a precisely synced cinematic **impact** (on frame 0)
- one short, readable **hook caption** (≤ 5 words); no second competing text block during the hook

The first 2 s must create curiosity **without misleading**.

## Rule 2 — Write three hooks before editing
For each topic write:
- **Hook A:** a shocking scientific fact
- **Hook B:** a terrifying hypothetical or consequence
- **Hook C:** an unexpected visual reveal

For each one, give: (1) the exact opening narration, (2) the first-frame composition, (3) the camera movement, (4) the
sound effect, (5) the on-screen text, and (6) why the viewer keeps watching.

Pick the strongest on clarity, immediacy, accuracy and visual impact. Reuse the losing hooks' best ideas later in the video.
Deliver as `THREE_HOOK_OPTIONS.txt`.

## Rule 3 — Retention through story (new information all the time)
| Time | Beat |
|---|---|
| 0–2 s | extraordinary opening hook |
| 2–8 s | establish the mystery, **with a real payoff by 8 s** |
| 8–20 s | first major revelation |
| 20–40 s | escalate with a surprising scientific detail |
| 40–60 s | the unexpected implication |
| 60–90 s | strongest payoff and a memorable conclusion |

Adapt the timing to the script length. No empty cliffhangers, no misleading claims, no disclaimer-sounding lines early on
(put labels on screen instead).

## Rule 4 — Cinematic editing
- Premium documentary look: real NASA/ESA footage where available and licensed (if blocked, use scientifically informed
  CGI, clearly labelled), plus original diagrams.
- A natural American male voice-over with natural pacing (never sped up to force a runtime).
- Dynamic but readable kinetic captions, inside the safe area.
- Motivated cuts, camera moves and transitions. Change the composition, scale and framing in every scene. No slideshow,
  no repeated background loop, no irrelevant stock, no generic AI-looking visuals.
- Sound effects synced to the events. Music supports suspense and never covers the speech.
- Pace by story and visual action, not a mechanical cut every second.

## Rule 5 — Retention quality gate (before the final render)
Render and inspect the **first 3 s separately** (frames at 0.0 / 0.1 / 0.5 / 1.0 / 2.0 / 3.0 s) and answer:
1. Does the first frame make sense on a muted phone?
2. Does the narration start immediately?
3. Is the opening sentence understood within 2 s?
4. Is there a clear visual event?
5. Do the first 8 s deliver a real payoff?
6. Are the captions visible and correctly timed?
7. Does every scene advance the story?

If any answer is no, revise and re-check before the full render.

## Rule 6 — Export and delivery
- TikTok: 1080×1920, 9:16, 30 fps, H.264 MP4, AAC, -14 LUFS.
- Deliverables: `FINAL_VIDEO.mp4`, `THREE_HOOK_OPTIONS.txt`, `FINAL_SCRIPT.txt`, `RETENTION_QA_REPORT.txt`.
- Inspect the actual rendered file (ffprobe + QC + frame review) before claiming it's complete.
- Never promise virality. Optimize for comprehension, curiosity, retention and shareability.
