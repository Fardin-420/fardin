SHORT DESCRIPTION
-----------------
Hacktoberfest 2026 lists an ElevenLabs offer: 1 month of the Creator tier, no credit card
required. In this NPC walkthrough I create a MyMLH account, complete the profile, register for
an MLH event, connect Hacktoberfest with MLH, and open the "codes and offers" page where the
ElevenLabs card lives.

Heads-up: the offer card says you need a VERIFIED PHONE NUMBER and a CONNECTED GITHUB ACCOUNT
on MyMLH before you can get the code. One code per account, codes don't stack. Offers,
eligibility and availability can change at any time, so check the current terms on the site.


STEPS SHOWN IN THE VIDEO
------------------------
1. Go to mlh.com and open the MyMLH sign-in / sign-up page.
2. Create your account: enter your first and last name.
3. Tick the Terms of Service / Privacy Policy box and tap "Sign Up!".
4. Complete your profile: the required demographic fields, your education (school, start date,
   or "Currently in school"), and your birthday. Tap Continue.
5. You're taken to the MLH event "Global Hack Week: Builders Week". Answer the registration
   questions (country, required agreements, how you heard about it, region, GitHub handle or
   N/A) and tap Submit. You'll see "Registered to attend!".
6. Open hacktoberfest.com and log in with your MLH account.
7. On the MLH screen "Authorize Hacktoberfest 2026 to access your account", tap Authorize.
8. On "Your Hacktoberfest codes and offers", scroll to "ElevenLabs - 1 Month Free".
9. The card lists the requirements: a verified phone number on MyMLH and a connected GitHub
   account on MyMLH. Once both are done, use "Get your code".

Note: the recording ends on the offer card. Verifying the phone, connecting GitHub, and
redeeming the code are not shown.
